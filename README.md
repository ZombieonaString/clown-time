NINTVG (Nine Inch Nails The Video Game) is a dramatic rhythm retelling of the history of the band "Nine Inch Nails" and its main artist "Trent Reznor". Experience the world shift as Trent, fight enemies in simple classic rhythm game fashion while making your music and developing your albums. Each album visibly changes the world around you, progressing the storyline and uncovering new baddies and secrets for you to discover!
